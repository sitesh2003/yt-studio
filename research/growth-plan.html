Growth plan for all three channels

Built from the study of the 19 Shorts in the "EVER THOUGHT WHY" playlist, our own numbers and the 13 Nov strategy (19 Nov 2026).

The setup: everything runs online on GitHub, free. Free online GPU time on Kaggle takes the heavy jobs. This laptop is the last option, used only when both are busy.

The five rules every video follows, on every channel

  1. A story, not a fact list. A hidden thing revealed, "you are" someone, or "you" in a real situation. The three explainer hits all do this; the eight beautiful Shorts without a story all stayed under 3,000 views.
  2. The first 2 seconds make people need the answer. A surprising true statement, or a question about something hidden. Never the title read out.
  3. Every picture shows what is being said, and changes every 3 to 4 seconds. No fillers. Only free, licensed footage; never other people's TV clips.
  4. The voice flows. Hits pause 1 to 2 times a minute; ours about 27.
  5. One question at the end, then a reason to come back: the last line leads into the first (it loops), or "part 2 tomorrow". No countdown.

Where each job runs

1. GitHub (first choice)

Free, unlimited minutes for our public repository; up to about 20 machines at once; no graphics card.

  • Scripts, fact checks, voices, captions, footage, editing, uploads (as now).
  • Manim animations and maps (already built).
  • 3D in Blender, split across machines: one GitHub machine renders a frame in about 6 seconds (measured 14 Nov), so a 40-second Short is split over 10 machines working together and done in about 15 minutes.

2. Kaggle (free online GPU)

About 30 hours of free graphics-card time a week; GitHub can start a Kaggle job and collect the result automatically.

  • Heavy 3D renders when they need more speed.
  • Open-source vision models that watch our finished frames and check them.
  • Faster voice generation.

3. This laptop (last option)

RTX 4050: a 3D frame in 0.65 seconds, about 9 times faster than one GitHub machine.

  • Used only if GitHub and Kaggle are both busy, or for the first hand-polished hero Shorts.

The free AI "brain": many providers, one switch

No single free service is enough, so the system tries them in order and moves on when one is busy or used up. Add a new free key to GitHub and it joins the list on its own. Free limits change often; these were checked today.

Each channel's place

Ever Thought Why? (English, US)

Hidden science in your body, kitchen and animals. Our best numbers already come from here.

  • 3D body and kitchen stories with the mascot; footage for animals and food.
  • Voice E2, flowing.

Why It Works (English, STEM)

How machines and everyday tech work inside. 3D cut-aways fit this channel best: engines, locks, microwaves, phones, bridges.

  • 3D machine sets and x-ray views, plus Manim for the physics.
  • Voice A1, flowing.

Aakhir Kyun? (Hindi)

Real Indian situations, told as a story. Fog on highways, trains, monsoon, cricket, festivals, food, the way 3D Wala Guru does it (527 thousand views on a 4,400-subscriber channel).

  • Hinglish with "तुम", a real event to open, what to do.
  • 3D road and city sets.
  • Voice H14 (Swara); one test of a lively male Hindi voice against it.

More reach, beyond the video itself

  • Series with "part 2 tomorrow" and their own playlists (playlists already automatic).
  • One look per channel: the same mascot, colours and caption style, recognisable in the first second.
  • Posting at each audience's hour: US evening for the English channels, 7 to 9 PM India time for Hindi.
  • Titles and descriptions in 5 languages (already on every upload), and YouTube's own auto-dubbing (a Studio setting you can switch on).
  • A weekly long video on the main channel (already built) for watch hours, which Shorts do not give.
  • Learn every week: our own retention data feeds the next scripts (running), plus a weekly check of the new hits you add to the playlist.

What only you can do (all free, no card)

  1. Make free accounts and create one API key at each: Groq, OpenRouter, Mistral, Cloudflare (account ID and API token), and Kaggle (Settings, then Create New Token; Kaggle asks for a phone check before giving GPU time).
  2. Paste each key into GitHub: the yt-studio repository, then Settings, Secrets and variables, Actions, New secret, with these names: GROQ_API_KEY, OPENROUTER_API_KEY, MISTRAL_API_KEY, CLOUDFLARE_ACCOUNT_ID, CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN, KAGGLE_USERNAME, KAGGLE_KEY.
  3. I never see or type the keys. Each one switches on by itself once it is there.
