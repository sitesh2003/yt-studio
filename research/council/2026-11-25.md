## Fact flags (both models)
- Cyanide inhibits **Complex IV (cytochrome c oxidase), NOT ATP synthase**; the gradient collapses. Do not say
  "stops in seconds" for every cell; time course depends on dose.
- Woodpecker: say the skull works like a **stiff hammer**; never "zero brain movement" or "immune to injury".
  (Gemini's round-2 table still said "shock absorption" – wrong per the 2022 paper; ignored.)
- Mosquito: the finding is skin carboxylic acids; never "blood group" or "sweet blood"; Gemini's "proboscis
  mechanics" note is off-topic.
- Mantis shrimp (if ever made): cavitation *may contribute* to damage; not "the bubble breaks every shell".
- Leidenfrost: the drop does not levitate forever; needs a hot enough surface.
- DNA packing (later book): do not show the neat 30 nm fibre as universal.
- Dog drinking: cite Gart et al. 2015 PNAS 112:15798 (or Crompton & Musinsky 2011).
