## Where both agreed (adopt)
1. **Frame 1 = the payoff**: the animal already lunging, the gauge already red; explain after.
2. **Named recurring series** per channel, so viewers recognise and return:
   - Ever Thought Why?: *Could It Actually Happen?*, *Nature's Engineering*, *What Would Break First?*
   - Aakhir Kyun?: *Ek Minute, Ye Kaise Hua?*, *Ghar Ki Science* (Indian homes/food/monsoon), *Desi Myth vs X-ray*
   - Why It Works: *One Law, One Experiment*, *What If We Remove This?* (failure first), *The Invisible Machine*
3. **Show the source on screen**: a 1-second card with journal + year when the big number lands ("receipts").
4. **Consistent visual language**: red = stress/danger, blue = baseline/cold, yellow = energy/variable, green = OK.
5. **Indian props** for Aakhir Kyun? (a Swift-like hatchback, ₹ coin, pressure cooker, ceiling fan).
6. **Judge by** viewed-vs-swiped, average % viewed, shares and subscribers per 1,000 views – per channel baseline.
7. **Long-form** versions of winners (watch hours are the more controllable monetisation route).

## Rejected (breaks our rules)
- "Make the viewer predict / countdown / freeze-frame quiz" (Gemini round 1, ChatGPT round 1) – deleted for hurting retention.

## Blunt critiques
- Gemini: spreading effort over three channels before one has a repeatable viral engine; pick a flagship.
- ChatGPT: optimise views/shares **per production hour**, not polish; a simpler 4-hour video can beat a 12-hour one.
