## Wet Runway पर Plane Hydroplaning से कैसे बचता है? 🤯
*2026-11-16 · How Things Work*

- Standard airport runway grooving specs require transversely cut channels roughly 6 mm (1/4 inch) wide and 6 mm deep spaced about 38 mm apart.
- Hydroplaning occurs when water pressure builds in front of a rolling tire, lifting it off the pavement and eliminating friction.
- Grooves provide high-pressure escape channels for water under heavy loads, allowing tire rubber to directly contact dry aggregate surface.

Sources:
- Deng et al. (2025), Lubricants. doi:10.3390/lubricants13020076 — supports: Hydroplaning occurs when water pressure builds in front of a rolling tire, lifting it off the pavement and eliminating friction.
