## Why Highway Guardrails Have Heavy End Boxes 🤯
*2026-11-14 · How Things Work*

- Guardrail end terminals (extruder terminals) flatten and curl steel W-beams upon impact.
- Plastic deformation of the guardrail absorbs the kinetic energy of a crashing vehicle.
- Extruder terminals are engineered to redirect the flattened beam away from the roadway to prevent vehicle spearing.

## Why Your Funny Bone Shocks You ⚡
*2026-11-14 · Body Whys*

- The ulnar nerve runs close to the surface at the elbow joint.
- Impact on the ulnar nerve produces a tingling or electric shock sensation down the forearm.
- Lack of thick muscle and fat padding over the ulnar nerve at the elbow makes it vulnerable to direct compression.

## Why Car Windshields Have Black Dots 🤔
*2026-11-14 · Everyday Whys*

- The black border and dots on a windshield are a ceramic paint called frit.
- Frit helps bake the adhesive that bonds the windshield to the car frame.
- The dot pattern (halftone) helps distribute thermal stress to prevent the glass from cracking due to temperature differentials between the dark ceramic and clear glass.

Sources:
- Öztürk et al. (2024), Bulgarian Chemical Communications. doi:10.34049/bcc.56.3.ik-m — supports: The black border and dots on a windshield are a ceramic paint called frit.
- Lundevall et al. (2018), Applied Adhesion Science. doi:10.1186/s40563-018-0111-6 — supports: Frit helps bake the adhesive that bonds the windshield to the car frame.

## How Mosquitoes Bite You Without Pain 🦟
*2026-11-14 · Animal Whys*

- Mosquitoes use a fascicle containing a labrum, mandibles, and maxillae to pierce skin.
- Mosquito saliva contains compounds with anesthetic properties that numb the feeding site.
- The bending and micro-serration of the proboscis reduce mechanical stress on skin mechanoreceptors.

Sources:
- Wahid et al. (2007), The Open Entomology Journal. doi:10.2174/1874407900701010001 — supports: Mosquitoes use a fascicle containing a labrum, mandibles, and maxillae to pierce skin.
