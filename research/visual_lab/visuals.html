Visuals samples

Two ideas to make the videos look better, made as samples first. Nothing here is in the daily videos yet. Watch them, then tell Claude which to switch on.

The clips are silent. In a real video the narration and captions play over them, and the captions sit in the empty band under the picture.

1. Pictures from the real study

Figures taken from open-access papers whose own licence is CC BY or CC0, the only licences that allow use on a monetised channel. Each figure is cropped to one panel so it reads on a phone, with a slow push-in, and the credit stays on screen.

  • Wrinkly fingers: the real test

    Under the line: “Scientists tested it: people gripped this sensor with dry, wet and wrinkly fingers.”

    Davis, PLoS One 2021, CC BY 4.0, Fig 1. Open the paper

  • Dinosaur blood: the fossil

    Mitchell et al., Sci Rep 2025, CC BY 4.0, Fig 1A, cropped. Find the paper

What the paper pictures can and cannot do

  • Works Biology, medicine, fossils, space photos: real photos, scans and simple diagrams from the study behind the fact.
  • Does not Physics and engineering topics (sirens, LEDs, GPS): the open paper library is life science, so searches return unrelated medical papers.
  • Rejected Lab-only figures (protein maps, gels, dense graphs), like the vaccine paper's figure: correct, but meaningless to a viewer. These need a check that throws them out.
  • Refused by rule Non-commercial (NC), no-derivatives (ND) and share-alike (SA) licences, and “free to read” papers with no open licence. The licence is read from the paper itself, not just the search listing.

2. A new stick character

One character with real joints, so it moves between poses instead of swapping drawings: every move eases in and out with a small overshoot, the body breathes when still, the face changes with the line, and objects react (the marble slides off the smooth hand and stays in the wrinkly one).

  • The wet marble

    Points, reaches, the marble slips (shock), thinks, grips it with wrinkly fingers, waves.

    6 seconds, drawn by code, no AI pictures.

  • The poses

    Wave, think, point, shock, cheer, all one character moving between them.

    8 poses so far; more can be added.

Honest level: this is clean and consistent, and moves far better than still drawings, but it is not a professional animator's work. Hands, walking and objects being handled are the hard part; those would come next if you like the direction.
