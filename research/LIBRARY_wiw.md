## Why Transformers Need Changing Current ⚡
*2026-11-14 · Physics*

- Transformers require a changing magnetic flux to induce voltage in the secondary coil via Faraday's law of induction.
- Direct current produces a static magnetic field after initial turn-on, creating zero steady-state induced voltage in the secondary coil.
- Alternating current continuously changes direction, producing the necessary changing magnetic field.
- Answer to Try this: A moving magnet changes the magnetic flux through the coil, which induces an electromotive force (voltage).
