## How Colour Vision Works: The Biology of Cone Cells Explained
*2026-11-16 · Biology*

- Human trichromatic color vision relies on three cone cell types (S, M, L) responding to overlapping spectral sensitivity curves.
- A red object reflects long wavelengths primarily, stimulating L-cones most heavily, but color perception is determined by the brain processing the ratio of activation across all three cone types.
- Protanopia is the absence or malfunction of long-wavelength (L) cone photopigments, causing red colors to appear dark or dull.

## When Does A Lens Flip The Image?
*2026-11-16 · Physics*

- Lenses converge light rays to a focal point.
- Light from the top of an object passes through the lens and is redirected to the bottom of the image plane, causing inversion.
- The virtual image produced within the focal length is upright and magnified.
- Cameras use lens convergence to focus real, inverted images onto sensors.

## Why Does a Siren Change Pitch? The Doppler Effect
*2026-11-16 · Physics*

- Sound wave crests bunch up ahead of a moving sound source and spread apart behind it.
- Perceived pitch is directly proportional to the frequency of sound waves hitting the ear.
- The speed of sound in air is determined by properties of the air (temperature, density) and is independent of source velocity.

## Why Don't Planets Fall Into The Sun?
*2026-11-16 · Physics*

- Planets stay in orbit because their tangential velocity is sufficient to counteract the gravitational pull of the Sun.
- If tangential velocity is removed, an object will fall directly toward the center of gravity.
- Orbits are effectively a continuous state of falling while moving fast enough to miss the central body.

Sources:
- Tsang (2025), Zenodo (CERN European Organization for Nuclear Research). doi:10.5281/zenodo.20150543 — supports: Orbits are effectively a continuous state of falling while moving fast enough to miss the central body.

## Why Does Soap Remove Grease?
*2026-11-15 · Chemistry*

- Water molecules are polar and do not mix with non-polar grease due to hydrophobic interactions.
- Soap molecules possess a polar hydrophilic head and a non-polar hydrophobic tail.
- Soap forms micelles around grease, allowing it to be rinsed away by water.
- Hot water increases molecular kinetic energy, speeding up the dissolving and melting of fats.

## Why Does Baking Soda Fizz With Vinegar
*2026-11-15 · Chemistry*

- Baking soda is sodium bicarbonate (a base).
- Vinegar contains acetic acid.
- The reaction creates sodium acetate, water, and carbon dioxide.
- Carbon dioxide gas causes the fizzing.
- Unreacted baking soda is basic and bitter.

- Mori et al. (2026), Chromatography. doi:10.15583/jpchrom.2026.016 — supports: Vinegar contains acetic acid.

## Why Do Objects Fall At The Same Rate? 😲
*2026-11-15 · Physics*

- Gravity accelerates all objects at the same rate in a vacuum regardless of mass (Equivalence Principle).
- F = ma, so a = F/m. Since F is proportional to mass (F = GmM/r^2), the 'm' in the numerator and denominator cancel out.
- Inertia is the resistance to acceleration, which is exactly why heavier objects are harder to move but also experience more gravitational force.

## How Salt Melts Ice: Chemistry Explained
*2026-11-15 · Chemistry*

- Water molecules on ice constantly shift between solid and liquid phases.
- Sodium and chloride ions dissolve into the surface liquid layer.
- Dissolved ions physically interfere with water molecules rejoining the crystal lattice.
- Freezing point depression lowers the temperature at which ice can remain solid.
- Eutectic point of NaCl-water is approximately -21.1 degrees Celsius.
